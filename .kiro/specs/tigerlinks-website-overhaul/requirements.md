# Requirements Document

## Introduction

TigerLinks is transitioning from a meme coin positioning to a game-first Web3 ecosystem. This comprehensive website overhaul requires content rewrites, structural changes, and technical improvements to align with the new strategic direction. The website must reinforce TigerLinks as a gaming platform where tokens amplify the experience rather than replace it.

## Glossary

- **TigerLinks**: The correct brand name (replacing all variants like TigerLinkz, Tigerlinkz)
- **Tiger**: The in-game currency earned through gameplay
- **TigerLinks_Token**: The Web3 token (replacing $TGBW references)
- **Game_First_Ecosystem**: A Web3 platform where gameplay and user experience take priority over token speculation
- **Closed_Loop_Economy**: An economic system where tokens are earned and spent within the platform
- **Content_Management_System**: The website system that manages and displays all content
- **SEO_System**: Search engine optimization components including metadata and schema
- **Navigation_System**: The website's menu and routing structure

## Requirements

### Requirement 1: Brand Identity Transformation

**User Story:** As a visitor, I want to see consistent TigerLinks branding throughout the website, so that I understand this is a professional gaming platform.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Content_Management_System SHALL replace all instances of "TigerLinkz" and "Tigerlinkz" with "TigerLinks"
2. THE Content_Management_System SHALL replace all "$TGBW" references with "TigerLinks Token"
3. WHEN displaying brand names, THE Content_Management_System SHALL use consistent casing for "TigerLinks"
4. THE Content_Management_System SHALL remove all meme coin terminology including "meme", "degenerate", and "hype-coin"
5. THE Content_Management_System SHALL remove aggressive language such as "torch scam coins" and "massive rewards"

### Requirement 2: Homepage Transformation

**User Story:** As a potential player, I want to understand that TigerLinks is a game-first platform, so that I know what to expect from the experience.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN a user visits the homepage, THE Content_Management_System SHALL display the headline "A game-first Web3 platform built for skill, progression, and longevity"
2. THE Content_Management_System SHALL display the subheadline "Fun comes first. Tokens amplify the experience — they don't replace it"
3. THE Content_Management_System SHALL provide a primary CTA button labeled "Play Free"
4. THE Content_Management_System SHALL provide a secondary CTA button labeled "Read Whitepaper"
5. THE Content_Management_System SHALL display proof bullets including "Free-to-play onboarding" and "Closed-loop economy balanced by sinks"
6. THE Content_Management_System SHALL include a three-step visual process: "Play Free → Earn Tiger → Spend Tiger"

### Requirement 3: About Page Content Replacement

**User Story:** As a visitor researching TigerLinks, I want to read the authentic story of the platform's development, so that I can understand its mission and values.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Content_Management_System SHALL replace the About page header with "The Tiger's Tale"
2. THE Content_Management_System SHALL display the subtitle "From conviction to execution — how TigerLinks was built to outlast the noise"
3. THE Content_Management_System SHALL replace the entire evolution timeline with the approved "TigerLinks — The Real Story" content
4. THE Content_Management_System SHALL remove the "Meet the Pack" section entirely
5. WHEN displaying the timeline, THE Content_Management_System SHALL format each quarter entry with proper headings and descriptions

### Requirement 4: Navigation Structure Reorganization

**User Story:** As a user, I want clear navigation paths based on my intent, so that I can quickly find relevant information.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Navigation_System SHALL provide a primary navigation for players including "Play", "How It Works", "Game Modes", "FAQ", and "Support"
2. THE Navigation_System SHALL provide secondary navigation for token information including "Token", "Economy", "Whitepaper", "Governance", and "Risks & Disclosures"
3. WHEN a user accesses the navigation, THE Navigation_System SHALL clearly distinguish between player-focused and token-focused paths
4. THE Navigation_System SHALL place token-related links in footer or secondary position
5. THE Navigation_System SHALL prioritize player experience links in the main navigation

### Requirement 5: Tokenomics Page Cleanup

**User Story:** As an investor or player, I want accurate and current tokenomics information, so that I can make informed decisions.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. IF market cap data is not real-time, THEN THE Content_Management_System SHALL remove the market cap display entirely
2. THE Content_Management_System SHALL remove "Staking Rewards" from the token utility list
3. THE Content_Management_System SHALL remove the active player count display sitewide
4. WHEN displaying token utilities, THE Content_Management_System SHALL only show verified and active features
5. THE Content_Management_System SHALL maintain accurate token allocation information

### Requirement 6: New Page Creation

**User Story:** As a user, I want comprehensive information about the platform's economy, roadmap, and legal aspects, so that I can fully understand the system.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Content_Management_System SHALL create an Economy page explaining Tiger currency, earning methods, spending sinks, and conversion availability
2. THE Content_Management_System SHALL create a Roadmap page with milestone-based structure using "Now/Next/Later" format
3. THE Content_Management_System SHALL create an FAQ page addressing common questions about pay-to-win mechanics, crypto requirements, and Tiger earning
4. THE Content_Management_System SHALL create a Legal/Disclosures page with risk disclosure, financial advice disclaimer, and jurisdiction limitations
5. THE Content_Management_System SHALL provide a downloadable Whitepaper labeled "Version 2.0 – Q1 2026" with last updated date

### Requirement 7: SEO and Metadata Optimization

**User Story:** As a search engine or social media platform, I want proper metadata and structured data, so that I can correctly index and display TigerLinks content.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE SEO_System SHALL update page titles to reflect game-first positioning
2. THE SEO_System SHALL update meta descriptions to emphasize gaming over token speculation
3. THE SEO_System SHALL update Open Graph titles and descriptions for social media sharing
4. THE SEO_System SHALL implement schema markup for Organization and SoftwareApplication
5. THE SEO_System SHALL ensure each page has exactly one H1 tag

### Requirement 8: Bot and Crawler Management

**User Story:** As a search engine crawler, I want appropriate access to TigerLinks content, so that I can properly index the website.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Content_Management_System SHALL allow access for Googlebot, Bingbot, Twitterbot, and Discordbot
2. THE Content_Management_System SHALL not block generic rendering engines
3. WHEN a bot requests content, THE Content_Management_System SHALL serve appropriate content without blocking legitimate crawlers
4. THE Content_Management_System SHALL maintain a robots.txt file with proper crawler permissions
5. THE Content_Management_System SHALL log crawler access for monitoring purposes

### Requirement 9: Content Tone and Compliance

**User Story:** As a regulatory-conscious platform, I want compliant content that avoids gambling implications, so that we maintain legal compliance.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Content_Management_System SHALL remove all references to "guaranteed profits" and "massive rewards"
2. THE Content_Management_System SHALL avoid gambling and wagering language throughout the site
3. THE Content_Management_System SHALL replace aggressive attack language with professional alternatives
4. WHEN displaying game features, THE Content_Management_System SHALL emphasize skill-based mechanics over speculation
5. THE Content_Management_System SHALL maintain humor and personality in appropriate contexts without compromising professionalism

### Requirement 10: Trust and Security Information

**User Story:** As a potential user, I want to understand the platform's security measures and trustworthiness, so that I feel confident using the platform.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Content_Management_System SHALL display current audit status or "in progress" indicator
2. THE Content_Management_System SHALL explain anti-bot and anti-abuse measures
3. THE Content_Management_System SHALL provide transparent token allocation summary
4. WHEN displaying security information, THE Content_Management_System SHALL use clear, non-technical language
5. THE Content_Management_System SHALL link to relevant security documentation and audit reports
